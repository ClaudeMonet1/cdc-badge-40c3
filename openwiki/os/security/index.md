# Files

- [Badge-to-badge BLE message transfer review](ble-message-transfer.md) - Code-level security and design review of cdc-badge-os's cdc_msg framework (used by GPG key exchange, vCard and plugins) — discovery and trackability, what the 6-digit pairing does and does not guarantee, the OFFER/chunk wire protocol, delivery to modules and plugins, MIME-handler registry semantics, limits, and a bug that appears to stop plugins from registering receive handlers.
- [FIDO2 / CTAP2 module review](fido2-ctap2.md) - Code-level review of cdc-badge-os's mod_fido2 — USB-only CTAP2 (advertised as 2.1) plus U2F, the getInfo it advertises, which commands are implemented, one-TROPIC01-slot-per-credential storage and sign counters, user presence and the badge-PIN-as-ClientPIN semantics, per-device attestation, and spec deviations including raw (non-DER) ES256 assertion signatures and the absence of hmac-secret.
- [GPG / OpenPGP module review](gpg-openpgp-module.md) - Code-level review of cdc-badge-os's mod_gpg — which keys live in the TROPIC01 versus software, OpenPGP card v3.4 compliance and deviations, the badge-to-badge cross-signing flow and what it does not verify, weaknesses in the received-key and self-certification stores, the 25-bit alchemical word fingerprint, and what this means for a web-of-trust keysigning-party plugin.
